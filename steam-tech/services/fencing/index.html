Palisade and steel fencing

Palisade and steel fencing, supplied, set in concrete and painted, along your whole boundary.

WhatsApp a photo Call [000 000 0000]

A fence goes up once and stays up for years, so we set every post in concrete and keep the lines straight even when the ground isn’t.

Palisade suits most boundaries. Mesh panels work where you want to see through. We’ll walk the boundary with you and suggest what works best for the site.

What’s included

  • Walking and measuring the boundary
  • Posts set in concrete
  • Panels fitted to follow the slope
  • Matching pedestrian or driveway gate if you need one
